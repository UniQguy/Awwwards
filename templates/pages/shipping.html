Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Demonstration store policy. This is an academic project; no actual sales occur and no legal liability is assumed.

1. Fulfillment Origin & Coverage

All customer orders are dispatched from our central electronics fulfillment hub in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We ship across India through certified courier logistics networks covering Tier 1 metropolitan centers, state capitals, and regional postal sectors.

Customers can verify real-time PIN code delivery serviceability directly on any product page or in the shopping cart prior to checkout.

2. Delivery Rates & Free Shipping Threshold

We believe in straightforward, transparent commerce without unexpected cart surcharges:

  • Orders ₹499 and above: Complimentary standard delivery to all serviceable Indian PIN codes.
  • Orders below ₹499: A flat, nominal shipping charge of ₹40 is applied to cover courier line-haul and packaging.

3. Transit Timelines

Standard transit durations from warehouse dispatch:

  • Tier 1 Metros (Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2 to 4 business days.
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities: 3 to 6 business days.
  • Regional & Island Territories (J&K, North-East, Andaman & Nicobar): 5 to 8 business days.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD) Guidelines

Cash on Delivery is available for eligible order amounts up to ₹50,000 at serviceable courier locations. Orders exceeding ₹50,000 require secure digital prepayment via card, UPI, or net banking.

5. Transit Insurance & Fragile Packaging

Sensitive electronics (cameras, glass-backed phones, high-performance headphones) are packed with multi-layer anti-static cushioning and tamper-evident security tape. All packages are insured against loss or severe courier transit damage until signature confirmation at the delivery address.
