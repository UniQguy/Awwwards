Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Demonstration store policy. This is an academic project; no actual sales occur and no legal liability is assumed.

1. 14-Day Hardware Inspection Window

Customers may initiate a return or exchange request within 14 calendar days of confirmed courier delivery.

Eligible items must be in brand-new, undamaged physical condition, complete with original manufacturer packaging, user manuals, warranty certificates, accessories, and unsevered security seals.

2. Serial Number Tracking & Verification

To prevent fraudulent return attempts and internal component swapping, all serialized consumer electronics (including smartphones, studio monitors, wireless headphones, and cameras) are tracked by unit serial numbers recorded at dispatch.

Returned units undergo a physical serial verification upon warehouse intake. Products exhibiting mismatched serial numbers, tampered IMEI barcodes, or physical liquid damage are returned to the sender without refund.

3. Pre-Dispatch Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled immediately from the order tracking screen or customer account dashboard prior to fulfillment center dispatch. Upon cancellation, reserved inventory is automatically released back to live stock and any online payment is reversed immediately.

4. Official GST Credit Note Generation

In strict accordance with Section 34 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017, whenever a previously invoiced order is cancelled or refunded, an official GST Credit Note is automatically issued in the system referencing the original invoice serial number and tax breakdown.

5. Refund Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are remitted to the original source payment method (credit/debit card, UPI, or net banking) via our payment gateway within 5 to 7 banking business days. Cash on Delivery refunds are disbursed via verified bank account transfer upon receiving NEFT/IMPS credentials.
