Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Demonstration store policy. This is an academic project; no actual sales occur and no legal liability is assumed.

1. Return Guidelines

This store is a demonstration environment for university evaluation. In a commercial deployment, returns would require items to be in undamaged physical condition with original manufacturer packaging and accessories.

2. Pre-Dispatch Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled from the order tracking screen or customer account dashboard prior to warehouse dispatch. Upon cancellation, reserved inventory is automatically released back to live stock and simulated online payments are reversed.

3. Credit Note Records

Whenever an order is cancelled or refunded, an official credit note record is generated in the system referencing the original invoice identifier and line-item tax breakdown.

4. Refund Processing Timelines

Demonstration refunds are simulated immediately upon cancellation. In production, refunds are remitted to the original source payment method within standard banking business days.
