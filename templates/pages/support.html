Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Cash on Delivery (COD) rules and limits?

Guest orders placed via Cash on Delivery are capped at ₹15,000 and require a 6-digit verification OTP sent to your contact email during checkout. Registered account members can place COD orders up to ₹35,000. To prevent automated spam, a maximum of 2 active COD orders are permitted per customer within 24 hours.

How do I cancel an order before it has shipped?

Navigate to the Track / Cancel Order page using your order number and billing email or phone. If your order is in Pending or Confirmed status, select your cancellation reason from the dropdown and submit. Reserved inventory is restored instantly, and an official cancellation notice and credit note are generated.

Are prices inclusive of GST? How do I get an invoice?

All catalog prices are inclusive of GST. An official GST Tax Invoice or Bill of Supply with appropriate HSN codes and tax breakdown is generated upon order placement and can be downloaded anytime as a PDF from your order tracking timeline.

What are the delivery timelines from the Ahmedabad fulfillment center?

Orders are dispatched from our Ahmedabad fulfillment center. Standard delivery is 2 to 3 business days within Gujarat and 4 to 6 business days across other states in India via surface logistics with real-time tracking scans.
