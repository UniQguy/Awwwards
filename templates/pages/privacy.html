Privacy Policy

Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Demonstration store policy. This is an academic project; no actual sales occur and no legal liability is assumed.

1. Data Minimization & Collection

ELECTROCART operates on strict data minimization principles. We collect only the contact information, shipping address, and phone number required to generate delivery manifests, calculate PIN-code serviceability, and issue official GST invoices.

We do not sell, rent, or lease personal customer information to third-party data brokers or marketing networks.

2. Data Protection Principles

  • Purpose Limitation: Customer data is utilized exclusively for demonstration order fulfillment, shipment tracking, and transactional email notifications.
  • Right to Erasure: Registered users may request account deactivation and anonymization of personal identifiers by contacting customer support.
  • Consent: Guest checkout requires affirmative submission of shipping details without background device tracking.

3. Payments & Financial Security

ELECTROCART does not store, process, or transmit raw credit card or debit card numbers on our infrastructure. All online payment transactions are processed through authorized payment aggregators using cryptographically signed webhooks and HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.

4. Client Storage & Cookies

We employ essential HTTP cookies strictly for:

  • Session persistence and shopping bag item preservation.
  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection on state mutations.

Browsing history features such as "Recently Viewed" items operate entirely within client-side browser localStorage and are never transmitted to our servers or third-party analytical endpoints.

5. Data Retention

Order history and demonstration invoices are retained temporarily during the evaluation session, after which transactional records are purged.
